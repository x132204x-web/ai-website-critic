# Report requirements

Write for the product owner and the team that will implement the improvements. Make the verdict understandable before the detailed findings.

Include, in order:

1. Review context and evidence limitations
2. Executive verdict
3. Scorecard
4. What is working
5. Prioritized findings
6. Conversion-flow analysis
7. Technical and accessibility signals
8. Recommended redesign direction
9. Quick wins
10. Longer-term improvements

Keep recommendations internally consistent. Preserve identified strengths. Distinguish observed facts from inference and do not repeat one issue across multiple sections. Prefer 5–8 consequential findings to an exhaustive list of minor defects.

The redesign direction should describe principles, hierarchy, content, and interaction changes—not copy another brand's visual identity. Quick wins should be feasible without rethinking the product. Longer-term work may involve research, positioning, information architecture, or design-system changes.
